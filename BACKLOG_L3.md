## 🇨🇷 Costa Rica

**Status:** Blocked

**Reason:** Costa Rica performs stem cell transplants but no public national bone marrow donor registry could be verified.

**Missing verification:**

* Public donor registry.
* Official registration pathway.
* Registration age.

**Next action:** Monitor future Ministry of Health or CCSS registry developments before promoting to L4.

## Promotion rule

A country only leaves this backlog after all four official checks are completed:

* official registry confirmed;
* official registration page confirmed;
* registration age verified from the registry;
* first registration step officially confirmed.
